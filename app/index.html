    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough, ~200h, 4/10 (PowerPyx). No mission replay — save at each chapter start (15 manual + 1 auto). Story first, then the stages below in order.

      The 100% checklist — gold “A Long Road”

      35 requirements in three groups. It becomes viewable only after the story, at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, but progress made during the story already counts. Nothing on it is missable; everything can be finished in free-roam after the story. Only five requirement types need everything: 39/39 gang operations, 33/33 underwater discoveries, 42/42 shooting-range golds, 42/42 drone time trials, 15/15 Bret Target Decks. Five more trophy-vs-100% comparisons (Kenton sets, Bret decks, underwater, animals, marked bills) live in these groups.

      Missables — what can actually go wrong

      Good news first: there is no mission replay in GTA VI, but the story trophies unlock automatically and cannot be missed, and PowerPyx and GTABase flag only one trophy as possibly missable.             How the list was checked

            Names, descriptions and grades come from the official 55-trophy list as PlayStation publishes it. Where guides disagree with each other — the final mission's chapter number, Tyrone's final quest name, whether the Chapter 1 balcony meditation is strictly required — this guide says so instead of picking a side.
