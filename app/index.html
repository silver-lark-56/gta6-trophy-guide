    Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). No mission replay and no New Game+, so save at the start of every chapter — 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. The order below is built from the official descriptions: story first, character finales and stranger strands while they are open, the one possibly-missable meditation early, then activities, collectibles and crime, and the 100% checklist last.

      The 100% checklist — gold “A Long Road”

      35 requirements in three groups. It becomes viewable only after the story, at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, but progress made during the story already counts. Nothing on it is missable; everything can be finished in free-roam after the story. Only five requirement types need everything: 39/39 gang operations, 33/33 underwater discoveries, 42/42 shooting-range golds, 42/42 drone time trials, 15/15 Bret Target Decks.

      Missables — what can actually go wrong

      Good news first: there is no mission replay in GTA VI, but the story trophies unlock automatically and cannot be missed, and PowerPyx and GTABase flag only one trophy as possibly missable.
